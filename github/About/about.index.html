A Little About ME


Hi! I'm Scout Santen, I was born and raised in Portland OR, but have spent the last 3 years of my life in Santa Barabara. I attended SBCC as a Graphic Design major where I learned a lot about Typography, editorial design, and a little bit of coding with Python. I'm new to SF and this is my first semester at SFSU! My major is visual communications with a focus on Graphic Design. My dream is to work as a Graphic Designer within the Fashion world and eventually work my way up to become a creative director and continue developing my skills as a designer.


I'm taking this class with the hope of getting more familiar with coding. I think with the rise of AI coding will be a important skill to know a little bit about, as it helps you stand out from other designers. Outside of class I enjoy exploring San Francisco, spending time at the beach, trying new foods, and getting outside with friends!
